💡Flash Option Chain [CRM] – Advanced Multi-Timeframe Option Chain & Confluence Engine💡

Live Option Chain Analysis | Multi-Timeframe Signals | Premium, Volatility & Market Structure

📌 Description

Flash Option Chain [CRM] is an advanced live option-chain analysis tool that combines option pricing, multi-timeframe signals, volatility insights and market-structure analysis in one compact TradingView dashboard. It helps traders evaluate Calls and Puts together for a broader market view.

✅ Core Capabilities

  • Live Call and Put option-chain display with configurable strike rows
  • Automatic symbol selection with support for NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY and MIDCPNIFTY
  • MCX support for GOLDM, SILVERM, CRUDEOIL and NATURALGAS
  • Broad F&O stock support with automated strike and contract selection.
  • Fully configurable option chain table, with customizable LTP, Intrinsic Value, Time Value, and all other columns.
  • Independent View, 5-Min, 15-Min and 1-Hour signal columns
  • Delta, Implied Volatility and Volume analysis
  • Dynamic Max Pain analysis across the selected strike range.
  • India VIX value and daily percentage-change display
  • Volatility-based Probable Range for market context.
  • Optional Current, Next and Cumulative Futures OI section with OI percentage changes
  • Automated expiry selection across supported contract cycles.

🎯 Multi-Timeframe Confluence

  • 5-Min: Short-term option-flow and buildup classification
  • 15-Min: Intermediate momentum and structure confirmation
  • 1-Hour: Higher-timeframe confirmation for Intraday/Crypto mode
  • View: Combines timeframe signals into directional Call/Put confluence
  • Signals include RC / RC+, Buy / Buy+, PB / PB+, Sell / Sell+

📊 Option Analytics

  • LTP: Current option price
  • Intrinsic Value: Calculated Call/Put intrinsic component
  • Time Value: Option premium remaining after intrinsic value
  • IV: Implied volatility estimated from the option price
  • Delta: Option sensitivity calculated from the estimated volatility
  • Volume: Session option volume displayed in compact format
  • PCR: Premium-weighted Call/Put ratio across the displayed band
  • Max Pain: Strike with the lowest calculated aggregate option pain

📈 Lower Chart CRM Engine

  • Quick-Scalp and Intraday/Crypto trading styles
  • Trend & SL Trailing Ribbon
  • Probable Trend Box with Bullish, Bearish and Sideways states
  • Automatic Entry, Stop-Loss and TP1/TP2/TP3 mapping
  • Configurable Entry/SL/TP labels and levels
  • VWAP and live price reference
  • Magnetic Reversal Zones with optional additional markers

🧭 Supported Assets

The indicator offers quick selection of major indices, commodities, and a wide range of Indian F&O stocks.

Supports NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, GOLDM, SILVERM, CRUDEOIL, NATURALGAS, plus a broad range of Indian F&O stocks for flexible market analysis.

🎯 Ideal For

  • Option Buyers: Compare premium movement, IV, Delta, Time Value and multi-timeframe signals.
  • Intraday Traders: Use the option chain with underlying trend, VIX and market-structure information.
  • Scalpers: Focus on 3-Min and 5-Min option-flow signals and the View confluence.
  • Option-Selling Traders: Evaluate Max Pain, PCR, IV, Volume, Straddle and broader market direction.
  • Multi-Timeframe Traders: Combine short-term and higher-timeframe confirmation in one dashboard.

🕒 Recommended Timeframes

  • 1min / 3min / 5min → Fast option-chain and scalping analysis with focused risk-management levels.

⚙️ Customizable Settings

  • Independent visibility controls for each option-chain data column
  • Selectable number of strikes: 2 + ATM + 2, 3 + ATM + 3, 4 + ATM + 4 or 5 + ATM + 5
  • Bottom-left or bottom-right option-chain placement
  • Adjustable option-chain text size
  • Auto Fetch Expiry with manual expiry override
  • Lower-chart trading style, ribbons, trend box and trade-level controls
